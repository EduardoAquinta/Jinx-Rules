# How to Play: Jinx

Whenever two people do or say the same thing at the exact same time, it's a race to call out the correct word. 
The **first person** to say the correct word wins.

## The Five word

1. JINX!

- **When to say it:** You both say the exact same real word at the same time. 
- Example: You both say "Pizza!" together.

2. JONX!

- **When to say it:** You both make the exact same sound, say the same made up word, or say two different words that have the same vowel sound at the exact same time. 
- Examples: You both sneeze together, you both say "splibber", or one says bubble and the other says "lunch".

3. JUNX!

- **When to say it:** You realise you both had the exact same thought at the same time. 
- Rule: you have to prove it. After calling "Junx!", both players must explain what they were thinking to confirm it really matched. No cheating by either player or the non-cheater automatically wins. 

4. JENX!

- **When to say it:** You both do the exact same physical move at the same time. 
- Example: You both reach for the same cup, scratch your nose, or trip over a step at the same time. 

5. JANX!

- **When to say it:** This is for anyone else watching. If two people have a Jinx, Jonx, Junx, or Jenx opportunity - and neither of them notices or reacts - this third person can shout "Janx!" to the steal the win. (You can even Janx a Janx, which would also assume a Junx has been missed.. this shit goes deep)

Tie-Breakers and The Meltdown

- Tied on Jinx? If you both shout "Jinx!" at the exact same time, keep going... because Jinx is a real dictionary word, you must shout "Jinx!" again to win.
